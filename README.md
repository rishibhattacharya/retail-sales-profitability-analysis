# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

An Excel-based business analytics project analyzing retail sales, profitability, discount patterns, and regional performance using the Sample Superstore dataset.

## 📊 Project Overview

The objective of this project was to analyze retail transaction data and identify patterns in sales performance, profitability, discounts, and regional performance.

The analysis was designed to answer questions such as:

* Which product categories generate the strongest profitability?
* Which categories have weaker profit margins?
* Does higher discounting appear to be associated with lower profitability?
* Which regions perform better or worse in terms of profit margin?
* What key business insights can be derived from the data?

## 🔍 Key Findings

### Category Performance

* **Technology** recorded the highest profit margin at **17.45%**.
* **Office Supplies** followed closely with a profit margin of **17.22%**.
* **Furniture** had the lowest profit margin at only **2.61%**, despite generating substantial sales.

### Discount & Profitability

* Furniture had the highest average discount at **17.30%**.
* Office Supplies had an average discount of **15.63%**.
* Technology had the lowest average discount at **13.15%**.
* The analysis suggests that higher discounting is associated with weaker profitability, particularly in Furniture.

### Regional Performance

* **West** recorded the highest regional profit margin at **14.98%**.
* **East** followed with **13.71%**.
* **South** recorded **11.93%**.
* **Central** had the lowest profit margin at **7.92%**, significantly below the overall margin of 12.56%.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Review discounting strategies for the **Furniture** category to improve margins.
2. Investigate the causes of weaker profitability in the **Central** region.
3. Maintain focus on **Technology** and **Office Supplies**, which demonstrate stronger margins.
4. Evaluate whether high discounts are generating enough additional sales to justify the reduction in profitability.
